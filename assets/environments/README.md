# Realm valley

`realm-valley.png` is original generated environment artwork (1536 × 1024),
created with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-21. No reference-game
assets were imported. It is shipped locally; the app makes no image-generation
requests at runtime. Existing warden and riftling assets were not modified.

The plate is intentionally character-free. Live agents, requests, selection,
labels, and controls are rendered independently. Courtyard coordinates are in
`src/renderer/src/game/realm-terrain.ts`. Changes to the plate's composition
require revalidating those coordinates and the Electron map captures.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D RTS environment background
for Realmkeeper, a dark-fantasy agent watch-room game. Generate a richly detailed
playable terrain plate, landscape 1536x1024 or larger, orthographic high-angle
isometric camera, no horizon. A contiguous inhabited kingdom valley, NOT floating
islands, NOT circles or a diorama on blank space. Painterly pre-rendered classic
RTS look with sharp readable architecture, excellent material textures,
comparable environmental richness to classic Warcraft and Age of Empires but
wholly ORIGINAL designs. Dense evergreen forest borders, weathered blue-gray
limestone cliffs, mossy grass, ochre dirt roads, a blue river bending down the
right third crossed by an old stone bridge, smaller brooks. Six spacious distinct
settlement courtyards arranged three columns by two rows, connected by believable
roads, all at identical world scale. Upper-left: violet crystal quarry/watchtower;
upper-middle: ivory citadel with indigo slate roofs and banners; upper-right:
timber market workshops with amber lamps. Lower-left: ruined woodland monastery;
lower-middle: black-stone forge with warm braziers; lower-right: teal tide sanctuary
and waterside docks. Buildings cluster on the northern/back edges of each
courtyard, leaving broad empty walkable ground in the center and foreground for
separately rendered agents. Small supporting buildings, carts, crates, fences,
gardens, stairs, walls, individual trees, rocks, shrines; coherent miniature
architecture at true RTS camera scale. Daylight with a cool twilight palette but
clearly visible terrain; warm windows, directional shadows to lower right. Fill
the whole rectangular image edge to edge with landscape. No people, creatures,
characters, units, UI, icons, circles, selection rings, text, labels, letters,
logos, or watermark. This image will be used as a real game terrain layer, not a
mockup.
